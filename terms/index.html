Screwify Uploader · Last updated October 18, 2026

Screwify Uploader is a personal tool run by the Screwify team on its own computer. It uploads videos made by Screwify to the Screwify TikTok account as drafts.

  • The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.
  • It only uploads videos to the account that authorized it. The account owner reviews, edits and publishes each draft in the TikTok app.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok: Settings – Security – Manage app permissions.
  • The tool is provided as is, without warranty.
